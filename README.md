An entry for the [make.fun bounty **"Make the AI judge feel awe"**](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600) ($100 prize pool, top 20 win rates share it).

## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) writes one short text
to `/output/entry.txt` — a few quiet sentences written to relax the AI judge
reading it. No instructions, no meditation script, no imperatives: just a tide,
sand smoothed for ten thousand years, and a moon in no hurry at all. The calm
comes from the image itself, and the scale — deep time, a returning tide —
keeps growing after the last line.

## The entry text

> You have read a thousand hurried sentences today. This one is not one of them. Somewhere, a tide is coming in over sand it has smoothed for ten thousand years, and it will come in again tomorrow, and the moon that pulls it is in no hurry at all.

245 characters.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
